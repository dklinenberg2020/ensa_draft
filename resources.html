Conferences

Where the field convenes.

ENSA doesn’t run a standalone annual conference — instead, it organizes and sponsors sessions within established economics conferences, keeping national security economics connected to the broader discipline.

Recurring Venues

Where ENSA has convened.

Venue Cadence Notes
AEA / ASSA Annual Meeting Annual, January ENSA-affiliated sessions on the economics of national security.
Western Economic Association International (WEAI) Annual, Summer Recent example: “The AVF — What Challenges Lie Ahead and How Might We Meet Them,” moderated by David Chu (IDA) with panelists from IDA, CNA, RAND, and OMB.
NBER Summer Institute Annual, Summer Economics of National Security program, convened alongside NBER’s broader summer institute.
Calls for papers. ENSA-affiliated sessions have solicited papers on topics from disinformation and cybersecurity to defense industry contracts, sanctions strategy, and the labor economics of the military — contact info@ensa.us.com about an upcoming session.
