Resources & Data

Data sets, research support, and conference sessions.

One of ENSA’s core activities is helping researchers find the national security data they need — and, where it isn’t already public, helping them identify the right authorities to request its release.

Conferences & Sessions

Where the field convenes.

ENSA doesn’t run a standalone annual conference — instead, it organizes and sponsors sessions within established economics conferences, keeping national security economics connected to the broader discipline.

Venue Cadence Notes
AEA / ASSA Annual Meeting Annual, January ENSA-affiliated sessions on the economics of national security.
Western Economic Association International (WEAI) Annual, Summer Program committee has included researchers from UC San Diego and RAND.
NBER Summer Institute Annual, Summer Overlapping research program on the economics of national security.
