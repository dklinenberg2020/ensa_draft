About ENSA

A shared home for national security economics.

Why the association exists, how it started, and who has led it.

Our Mission

Advance the field, and put it to use.

ENSA is a professional organization dedicated to promoting excellence in national security economics research. ENSA provides a forum for new ideas and empirical results of economic research applied to national security, defense resource management, and public policy relating to national security and defense.

Through a set of professional activities, ENSA aims to advance national security economics research, to achieve widespread recognition for the field of national security economics, and to enhance national security by providing evidence and expertise for the development of public policies. ENSA is a professional organization and is not affiliated with any government organization.

“National security was the single largest component of GDP, sparsely studied by economists.” — Martin Feldstein, ENSA Founding President

Who it’s for. Academic economists, researchers at military academies, and professionals at defense-related think tanks and FFRDCs — anyone bringing rigorous economic analysis to bear on national security questions.
History

From a Harvard seminar to a national association.

  • The Seminar

    Martin Feldstein runs a long-standing seminar on the economics of national security at Harvard, drawing attention to how large a share of GDP — and how understudied a field — national security really was.

  • Founding

    Feldstein establishes ENSA as a national organization to formalize that community, with founding representation from three Federally Funded Research and Development Centers — IDA, CNA, and RAND — alongside academic and government-adjacent researchers.

  • Building the Field

    ENSA becomes a recurring presence at the Western Economic Association International conference, while overlapping with NBER’s Economics of National Security research program and summer institute.

  • Today

    The association continues as a member-run professional community, with a board and officers elected periodically to guide its research, conference, and membership activities.

Leadership

Founders & Board

ENSA is governed by a member-elected board and officers, drawing on leadership from its founding FFRDC partners, West Point, and academic economics departments.

EB

Eli Berman

President & Founding Board Member · UC San Diego

Professor of economics at UC San Diego and Research Director for International Security Studies at the UC Institute on Global Conflict and Cooperation. Leads the Economics of National Security group at NBER.

SJ

Sarah John

Secretary & Board Member · IDA

Assistant Division Director, Cost Analysis and Research Division at the Institute for Defense Analyses. An ENSA member since its founding.

MM

Michael Meese

Founding Board Member

President of the American Armed Forces Mutual Aid Association (AAFMAA); retired U.S. Army Colonel and former Head of the Department of Social Sciences at West Point.

JW

Jennie W. Wenger

Board Member · RAND

Senior Economist and Research Department Director for Economics, Sociology, and Statistics at RAND, studying military compensation, recruiting, and veterans’ labor outcomes.

BK

Bill Komiss

Board Member · CNA

Research Program Director for Energy, Infrastructure & Environment Resources and Force Readiness at CNA, specializing in the economics of energy and infrastructure.

CW

Col. Carl J. Wojtaszek

Board Member · West Point

Director of the Economics Program at West Point and fifth Director of the Army’s Office of Economic and Manpower Analysis (OEMA) in its 40-year history.

MK

Mike Kofoed

Board Member · University of Tennessee, Knoxville

Assistant Professor of Economics and IZA Research Fellow studying the economics of veteran education and health, including the Post-9/11 G.I. Bill.

MF

Martin Feldstein

Founding President

Harvard economist whose national-security seminar became the basis for ENSA’s founding.
