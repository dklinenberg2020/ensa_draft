What We Do

A forum, a data hub, and a community.

Our Origins

“National security is the single largest component of federal spending, yet one of the least studied by economists.” — Martin Feldstein, ENSA Founder

ENSA grew out of a seminar on the economics of national security that Martin Feldstein ran for years at Harvard. Recognizing how little of the field was organized into a shared body of work, he brought together researchers from three Federally Funded Research and Development Centers — IDA, CNA, and RAND — along with academic and government partners, including founding member Eli Berman (UC San Diego), to formalize the association. Berman now serves as ENSA’s president.

ENSA is a professional organization and is not affiliated with any government organization.
