Membership

Join the community.

ENSA membership is open to academics, FFRDC and think-tank researchers, military academy faculty, and government analysts working on the economics of national security.

Who Joins ENSA

A professional home across sectors.

🏫

Academics

University economists and PhD researchers studying defense, conflict, and security policy.

🏢

FFRDC Researchers

Analysts at institutions such as IDA, CNA, and RAND working on defense resource management.

🌱

Military Academies

Faculty and researchers at service academies and defense-affiliated institutions.

🏢

Policy & Government

Analysts at defense-related think tanks and government offices informing public policy.

How to Join

A simple process.

  • 1. Reach Out

    Email info@ensa.us.com or use the contact form to request current membership categories and dues.

  • 2. Confirm Eligibility

    We’ll confirm the category that best fits your institutional affiliation and research focus.

  • 3. Join the Community

    Once confirmed, you’ll be added to the membership roster, mailing list, and voting rolls for board elections.
