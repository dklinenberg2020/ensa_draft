Membership

Join the community — it’s free.

ENSA membership carries no dues and is open to academics, FFRDC and think-tank researchers, military academy faculty, and government analysts working on the economics of national security.

Who Joins ENSA

A professional home across sectors.

🏫

Academics

University economists and PhD researchers studying defense, conflict, and security policy.

🏢

FFRDC Researchers

Analysts at institutions such as IDA, CNA, and RAND working on defense resource management.

🌱

Military Academies

Faculty and researchers at service academies and defense-affiliated institutions.

🏢

Policy & Government

Analysts at defense-related think tanks and government offices informing public policy.

Membership

One membership, free for everyone.

ENSA has a single membership — no dues, no tiers. Every member gets the same access.

ENSA Membership

Free · no dues
  • Full access to the research library
  • Voting rights in board elections
  • Conference session announcements and priority notice for AEA / WEAI sessions
  • Ability to contribute research to the library
  • Community mailing list
Join Free
Open to all sectors. Academics, FFRDC and think-tank researchers, military academy faculty, and government analysts all join under the same membership — institutions are welcome to have multiple affiliated members.
How to Join

A simple process.

  • 1. Reach Out

    Email info@ensa.us.com or use the contact form — there’s no application fee or dues at any step.

  • 2. Confirm Eligibility

    We’ll confirm your institutional affiliation and research focus fit ENSA’s membership.

  • 3. Join the Community

    Once confirmed, you’ll be added to the membership roster, mailing list, and voting rolls for board elections — free of charge.

Ready to join ENSA?

Membership is free — send us a note and we’ll get you set up.
